Privacy Policy — Tikyetok

Last updated: May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tikyetok (the "App") handles personal data. The App is a personal-use automation tool operated by a single individual creator for that creator's own TikTok account. It is not a multi-user service.

1. Data the App Accesses

To perform its function, the App accesses the following data through the TikTok API, solely for the operator's own TikTok account:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) — used to identify the connected account.
  • Video upload and publishing endpoints — used to post videos created by the operator.
  • Aggregated video performance metrics (views, likes, shares, completion rate, follower count) — used by the operator to evaluate content performance.

The App does not access data of any other TikTok user.

2. Data the App Does NOT Collect

  • The App does not collect personal data from end users of TikTok or any third party.
  • The App does not track, profile, or share data with advertisers.
  • The App does not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

3. Where Data Is Stored

The App runs locally on the operator's personal computer. Authentication tokens, configuration, and performance logs are stored in local files on that machine and are not transmitted to any third-party server other than the official APIs the App calls (TikTok, Anthropic, ElevenLabs).

4. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • TikTok — for authentication, video publishing, and analytics. See TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — for AI content generation. See Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs — for AI voice synthesis. See ElevenLabs Privacy Policy.

The operator only sends to these services the content needed for the requested task (text prompts, generated scripts, video files). No personal data of TikTok end users is sent.

5. Data Retention

Authentication tokens are kept locally only as long as needed to operate the App and can be revoked at any time from the operator's TikTok account settings. Performance logs are kept locally for the operator's own historical analysis and can be deleted at any time.

6. Security

API keys and authentication tokens are stored in local configuration files on the operator's machine. The operator is responsible for the security of that machine. Tokens are transmitted only over HTTPS to the official APIs of the services listed above.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Because the App is operated by a single individual for that individual's own account, GDPR rights apply to data the operator controls about themselves. The operator can:

  • Revoke the App's access at any time via TikTok account settings.
  • Delete locally stored data by removing the App's working directory.
  • Request information about data held by the third-party providers listed above directly from those providers.

8. Children

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
